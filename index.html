I spent the better part of twelve years working at the intersection of technology, marketing, and growth — the kind of work where you're equal parts strategist, builder, and person who stays up too late staring at funnel dashboards. I co-built a marketing agency to $60K+ MRR, led nationwide recruitment campaigns for loan officers, rebuilt conversion systems for an e-commerce golf marketplace, and ran digital campaigns for county-wide sales teams that needed consistent inbound demand and not just pretty impressions. I was hands-on with everything: strategy, copy, creative, tracking, automation. If it touched revenue, I wanted to understand it.

For a while, I thought the move was to build something of my own. And I tried. Entrepreneurship taught me a lot — about systems, about people, about the gap between a good idea and a working business. Somewhere along the way I realized I was more energized by building inside something great than by carrying the full weight of owning it. That's not a retreat. That's just knowing yourself.

Today I lead regional operations at Cabana, where I oversee field teams, client experience, and the work of helping build what will become the first national brand in pool service. What's happening here feels like lightning in a bottle and I'm grateful to be part of it.

Outside of work, my life is anchored by faith and family. I'm a devoted Catholic — my faith isn't a footnote, it's the foundation everything else is built on. It shapes how I lead, how I show up for my team, and how I treat the people around me.

My other priority is becoming the best husband I can be to my fiancée, who, as it happens, also works alongside me at Cabana. We're building something meaningful both at work and at home, and that's not a coincidence. It's a choice.

For now, that means making the world better, one pool at a time.
